# FreshMart: Sales, Profit \& Customer Performance Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive **end-to-end Power BI dashboard** for FreshMart, one of India's largest retail hypermarket chains, providing insights into sales trends, profitability, product performance, customer behaviour, and store and delivery operations across India.

> 📊 Power BI Desktop | 5 report pages | 3 years of data (2023-2025) | 68,000 orders | 90 stores | 40 states

\---

## 📌 Project Overview

FreshMart is one of India's largest retail hypermarket chains, selling groceries, food \& beverages, apparel, footwear, electronics, personal care, and home \& kitchen products across the country. This dashboard provides a **360° view** of business performance, enabling category managers, store/regional heads, and leadership to make faster, data-backed decisions on inventory, pricing, marketing, and store operations.

**Dataset Scope:**

* **68,000 orders** from **40,668 unique customers**
* **90 FreshMart stores** across **58 store cities** and **40 states** (customers from **77 cities**)
* **6,000 products** in **25 categories** and **117 sub-categories**
* **56 brands** across **8 store formats**
* **3-year span:** January 2023 – December 2025

### 2\. **Sales \& Revenue Overview**

Sales performance dashboard tracking revenue, profitability and trends.

**KPIs:**

* **Total Orders:** 68K
* **Total Sales:** ₹579M (₹57,87,31,956)
* **Total Profit:** ₹69M (₹6,86,62,855)
* **Profit Margin:** 11.86%
* **Loss Rate:** 1.60%

**Visualizations:**

* Sales Trend Over Time (area chart by year: 2023, 2024, 2025)
* Sales by State (column chart)
* Profit by Quarter and Year (line chart)
* Sales by Category (horizontal bar)
* Loss Rate % by Category (horizontal bar)
* Sales and Profit by Month (combo chart)

**Filters:** Year, State, Category

**Key Insights:**

* Yearly sales grew from ₹156.6M (2023) to ₹191.4M (2024) and ₹230.8M (2025)
* Profit margin stayed steady at about 11.9% in every year
* October and November are the strongest months (₹63.8M and ₹65.0M); January is the weakest (₹36.1M)
* Karnataka, Rajasthan and Maharashtra lead state sales at about ₹23M each
* Kitchen \& Home Essentials has the highest loss rate (2.06%)

### 3\. **Product \& Category Performance**

Deep dive into pricing, discounts and product-level profitability.

**KPIs:**

* **Total Quantity Sold:** 373,417 units
* **Total Products:** 6,000
* **Average Unit Price:** ₹1,604
* **Average Discount %:** 3%
* **Total Cost:** ₹510M

**Visualizations:**

* Sales by Category and SubCategory (treemap)
* Profit by Brand (column chart)
* Sales by PackSize (donut chart)
* Sales by ProductName (top products)
* Quantity Sold by Category (column chart)
* Sales by Sub-Category (ranked bar)

**Filters:** Category, Brand, Sub-Category

**Key Insights:**

* Staples \& Grains is the top category by both sales (₹34.4M) and units sold (22,666)
* Bags, Watches \& Eyewear (₹31.2M) and Sports, Pets \& Auto (₹30.3M) follow
* Top sub-categories are Pet Accessories (₹7.7M), Popcorn and Educational Toys (₹6.8M each)
* Sanskar Kids (₹1.76M), Whitmore \& Co (₹1.50M) and Nirmal Care (₹1.46M) are the most profitable brands
* Profit is spread widely: the top 10 of 56 brands earn only about 21% of total profit
* Pack sizes sell evenly: Pack of 2 (13.6%), 1 Unit (13.1%), Pack of 6 (12.9%) and Pack of 3 (12.8%)
* Category margins are tightly grouped between 11.6% and 12.1%

### 4\. **Customer Insights**

Customer demographics, buying patterns and membership analysis.

**KPIs:**

* **Unique Customers:** 40,668
* **Average Age:** 43.48 years
* **Total Orders:** 68,000
* **Average Order Value:** ₹8,511
* **One-Time Buyers:** 21,900

**Visualizations:**

* Average Discount % by Membership Tier
* Sales by AgeGroup (18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, 66+)
* Sales by City (top 3: Bhubaneswar, Ahmednagar, Agartala)
* Sales by Membership Tier
* Membership Tier Summary table (average discount and customer count)
* Top Customers table (CustomerID, name, sales, orders)

**Filters:** Gender, Membership Tier, Age Group

**Key Insights:**

* Average customer age is 43.5 years; the 56-65 group spends the most (₹113M) and the 66+ group the least (₹45M)
* Sales are split evenly between female (50.2%) and male (49.8%) customers
* Bronze is the largest tier (14,390 customers, ₹208M sales) with the lowest average discount (1.50%)
* Diamond is the smallest tier (2,038 customers, ₹27.8M sales) but gets the highest average discount (7.99%)
* Over half of customers (53.9%) have ordered only once, an opportunity for retention campaigns
* The top three customer cities each contribute about ₹8.4M, so sales are spread across many cities

### 5\. **Store \& Delivery Performance**

Operational metrics covering store formats, fulfilment and delivery speed.

**KPIs:**

* **Average Delivery Days:** 1.54
* **Cancellation Rate:** 6.09%
* **Returned Rate:** 4.94%
* **Delivered Rate:** 86.0%
* **Total Stores:** 90

**Visualizations:**

* Sales by StoreType (column chart)
* Orders by Year and Status (stacked column: Cancelled, Delivered, Pending, Returned)
* Average Delivery Days by Store Type
* Sales by StoreCity (top 3: Chandigarh, Hyderabad, Kohima)
* Sales by StoreState (Telangana, West Bengal (South), Chandigarh lead)
* Monthly Orders by Status (line chart)

**Filters:** Store Type, Store State, Status

**Key Insights:**

* FreshMart Central generates about 30% of total sales, ahead of Max (19%) and Nook (12%)
* Delivery speed depends on store format: Nook, Go and Local average 1 day, Central 1.5 days, Max and Plaza 2 days, Bulk and Grand about 2.5 days
* Order status mix is stable every year: about 86% delivered, 6% cancelled, 5% returned and 3% pending
* About 14% of orders are not delivered, the main operational improvement opportunity
* Telangana is the top store state (₹38.2M); Chandigarh (₹25.9M) and Hyderabad (₹25.2M) are the top store cities

## 💡 Key Business Insights

✅ **Revenue \& Growth**

* 3-year revenue: ₹579M (₹57.87 Cr) with YoY growth of 20.57% in 2025
* Sales grew 22.2% from 2023 to 2024 and 20.6% from 2024 to 2025
* Profit: ₹69M (₹6.87 Cr) at a stable 11.86% margin
* Only 1.60% of orders (1,091 orders) were sold at a loss

✅ **Operational Performance**

* Average delivery time: 1.54 days
* Delivery success: 86.0%
* Cancellations (6.09%) and returns (4.94%) together affect about 11% of orders
* Delivery speed varies by store format, from 1 day to about 2.5 days

✅ **Product Mix**

* 6,000 products across 25 categories and 117 sub-categories
* Top categories: Staples \& Grains, Bags, Watches \& Eyewear, Sports, Pets \& Auto
* Sales and profit are evenly spread across categories and brands, with no single dependency
* Average discount is 3%, and pack sizes sell in similar proportions

✅ **Customer Profile**

* 40,668 customers with an average age of 43.5 years and an average order value of ₹8,511
* 21,900 one-time buyers (53.9%): a clear retention opportunity
* Higher membership tiers get higher discounts (Diamond 7.99% vs Bronze 1.50%) but are much smaller groups
* UPI is the most-used payment mode (31.8% of orders), followed by credit card (18.1%) and debit card (16.1%)

✅ **Geographic \& Store Performance**

* 90 stores across 58 cities and 40 states
* Telangana, West Bengal (South), Chandigarh and Rajasthan lead store-state sales
* Store formats differ widely: Central (₹174M) vs Grand (₹25M)

## 🚀 How to Use

### Prerequisites

* **Power BI Desktop** (free download from [Microsoft](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/en-us/desktop/))

### Steps

1. **Download or clone the repository**

```
   freshmart-dashboard/
   ├── README.md
   ├── LICENSE
   ├── freshmart\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_sales\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_analysis.pbix
   ├── data/
   └── screenshots/
   ```

2. **Open the dashboard in Power BI Desktop**

   * Double-click `freshmart\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_sales\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_analysis.pbix`
   * Or: File → Open → Select the .pbix file
3. **If the data source cannot be found**

   * Go to Home → Transform data → Data source settings → Change Source
   * Select `data/FreshMart\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_Sales\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\_Analysis.csv`, then click **Refresh**
4. **Navigate \& Explore**

   * Move between the 5 pages using the tabs in the top bar
   * Use slicers to filter by Year, State, Category and more
   * Click on bars or segments to cross-filter other visuals
   * Hover over charts to see exact values
